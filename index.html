03 / METHODOLOGY

How to read this observatory

OpenAlex and Crossref metadata are searched on a schedule. Items are screened by keywords, deduplicated, and grouped by research theme. Curated initial references and newly discovered metadata are explicitly distinguished.

Attention fragmentation is not equivalent to reduced cognitive effort, critical-thinking self-reports, or technology engagement. Studies of notifications and task switching are adjacent evidence unless they examine AI directly.

Source reading cards use provided descriptions and metadata only. Visuals show possible mechanisms and metadata coverage, not empirical effect-size graphs. A missing abstract or unavailable full text never justifies inventing results.

The update timestamp documents the last successful index refresh. The site is refreshed from the repository, not by searching the web on every page load.
