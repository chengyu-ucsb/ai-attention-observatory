ABOUT THE OBSERVATORY / 03

An evolving research map.

What we're tracking.

This project follows how AI and digital systems affect attention allocation, cognitive offloading, sustained focus and critical thinking, together with design interventions that might protect these capacities.

Direct AI studies and adjacent research on notifications or multitasking are deliberately distinguished.

What a research card means.

Automated records are screened using source metadata. Some open-access papers contain short, labeled excerpts from licensed full-text sections. Concept diagrams are interpretive aids, not reported effect sizes or faithful reconstructions of original figures.

Always open and evaluate the full publication before treating a result as established.

Updates & storage.

OpenAlex, Crossref, and news RSS are queried each week. The update date records the most recent successful indexing run, not a real-time web search. Source errors can mean partial coverage. The open repository stores metadata and short excerpts rather than publisher PDFs.
